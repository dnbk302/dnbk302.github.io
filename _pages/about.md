Welcome! I’m Sumanth—a builder, a problem-solver, and a lifelong learner. My journey in technology has taken me from the vibrant classrooms of India to the innovative labs of New York, and into the heart of cloud-native engineering.

I believe the best software is invisible: it quietly empowers, adapts, and endures. My work is guided by curiosity, a love for elegant solutions, and a drive to make technology feel effortless for others.

---

## A Few Things About Me

- **I thrive at the intersection of systems and people.** Whether it’s designing robust backend services, orchestrating distributed systems, or mentoring peers, I’m most engaged when solving real problems that matter.

- **I value clarity and craft.** From code to communication, I strive for simplicity, precision, and a touch of delight.

- **I’m always learning.** My academic path (M.S. in Computer and Information Science, University at Buffalo; B.Tech, SASTRA University) and industry experience (SS&C EZE, both in India and the US) have taught me that technology is never static—and neither am I.

---

## What I Do

- **Engineer scalable platforms.** I’ve helped modernize investment platforms, migrate compute planes to Kubernetes, and automate deployments for reliability and speed.
- **Build for security and performance.** From secure token storage on mobile to fine-grained access controls in databases, I care about the details that keep systems safe and fast.
- **Collaborate and mentor.** I’ve led teams, graded algorithms courses, and contributed to open-source and research projects—because sharing knowledge is as important as gaining it.

---

## Beyond the Code

I’m fascinated by the stories behind every system—how people use them, where they break, and how they can be better. I share what I learn on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@stumbled001/videos), hoping to spark curiosity in others.

If you’re passionate about building things that last, or just want to talk tech, algorithms, or the art of learning—let’s connect.
